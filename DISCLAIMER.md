# Disclaimer

## Not immigration advice
immicite provides information about Canadian immigration rules. It is not legal or
immigration advice, and it never decides whether a particular person qualifies for
anything. Answers can be incomplete, wrong or out of date. Always verify against the
official source before relying on one.

## Built for licensed practitioners
immicite is a research aid for licensed immigration professionals, who remain responsible
for the advice they give. It is not a substitute for one. If you need advice about your
own situation, consult an authorized representative or IRCC's official tools.
